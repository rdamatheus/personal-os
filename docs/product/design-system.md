# Experience & Design System

## Direction
**Quiet cockpit.** Dark, spacious, intentional and calm. The UI should feel like a premium instrument panel rather than corporate project-management software.

## Personality
- confident, not aggressive;
- inspiring, not motivational-poster-like;
- dense when needed, spacious by default;
- warm enough to feel personal;
- neutral toward misses and setbacks.

## Home hierarchy
1. What matters now.
2. Three primary outcomes.
3. Current state: focus, energy, mood, stress.
4. Next routine / commitment.
5. Inbox and unresolved decisions.
6. Optional pattern insight.

## Copy principles
Prefer “What matters today?”, “This can wait.” and “A pattern may be emerging.” Avoid guilt, streak punishment and productivity slogans.
